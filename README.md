### 🚧 Currently Working On
- **[Confederate](https://github.com/HIHRAIM/Confederate)** — Discord ↔ Telegram bridge: relays messages between channels, threads and forum topics; brings in recent changes from any MediaWiki site, Fandom Discussions, Bluesky, YouTube and public Telegram channels; turns other Telegram bots' DMs into support threads on both platforms.
- **[Confederate Guard](https://github.com/HIHRAIM/Confederate-Guard)** — Discord moderation bot for networks of allied servers: bans link and file spammers in guarded channels, shares bans across the network with human review of automatic ones, verifies members by activity across servers and runs a joint appeal flow with Confederate.
- **[Democrate](https://github.com/HIHRAIM/Democrate)** — Discord & Telegram coordination bot for unions of wiki communities: parties and governing bodies, Fandom-profile verification, quizzes through dialogue, wiki anniversaries, Wiki Olympiad voting and a cross-community economy with banks, currencies, production and enterprises.
- **[Confederate Fandom](https://github.com/HIHRAIM/Confederate-Fandom)** — Discord & Telegram bot for Fandom wiki maintenance, built on Pywikibot: find and replace, Russian and Ukrainian spelling fixes, categories, interlanguage links, redirects, renames and more on any Fandom wiki, once or on a schedule, with a plan and an optional diff preview before the first edit.
